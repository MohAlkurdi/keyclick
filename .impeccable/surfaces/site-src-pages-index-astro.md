# Surface: KeyClick landing page (site/src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: MacBook typers who want a mechanical keyboard feel. Action: download the free app from GitHub Releases. Proof: the visitor types on the page and hears the real recordings of each key. Constraints: no invented claims, users, or numbers; plain English for non-native readers; tip link hidden until a tip URL exists.

History: the first build (a "Sample Pack" device-panel page) was rejected by the user as generic AI output: a split hero with a widget, a spec table, three steps and a dark CTA band. This contract replaces it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is your keyboard. A MacBook keyboard fills the first screen; turning KeyClick on raises its flat keys into mechanical keycaps, and every key you press sinks and plays its own recording. It refuses the template of split hero, widget, feature grid and CTA band.

OWN-WORLD: A night desk: graphite ground (#0a0a0b), a space-black deck, dark keycaps with warm white legends. The only colour is the selected switch's stem colour (brown, red, blue, purple, smoke), glowing from under the pressed keycap and marking the selection. Type is Hanken Grotesk, tight and medium weight. Real Mac legends (⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧), hairlines, and no cards.

STORY: See the keyboard, type, and hear it. Flip KeyClick off and hear the silence. See that every key has its own waveform. Learn that it sits in the menu bar and cannot read what you type. Download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A minimal nav. A centred two-line headline, one support line, a white Download button and the requirements. The echo line of what you type. A full-width keyboard in perspective, and under it the On/Off switch and five switch stems.

FORM: A self-directed redesign after the user's rejection (the "prove, don't claim" keyboard); replaces seed 1151358f. Signature interaction: on load the flat keys rise into keycaps in a left-to-right wave; key presses sink the cap and light the stem colour underneath; the On/Off switch morphs the keys flat ↔ mechanical.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
