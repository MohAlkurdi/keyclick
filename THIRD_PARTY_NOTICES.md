# Third-party notices

## Keyboard recordings

Everything under `Sounds/` is cut from the built-in Cherry MX and Topre sound packs of
[Mechvibes](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes) (commit `326252a13e7bef4f1c35d08ef0189b5af6f8ba02`,
`src/audio`), by Hai Nguyen. Each key was split into a press and a release sound and resampled to 48 kHz by
`tools/slice_mechvibes.py`, using the per-key timings from
[mechvibes-dx](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes-dx) (commit `a13c4181`). Switch names identify
the recordings and imply no endorsement.
